# Decision log

Decisions the owner has made about which plans to pursue and how. Newest first. Each plan
file's "Decisions still open" section lists what remains.

## 2026-10-20: Pursue plans 01 and 03. Park plans 02 and 04.

**Decision.** Build the niche data product (plan 01) as the revenue business, and the
templates and skills work (plan 03) as open-source marketing for Steerwell, the owner's AI
consulting company. The plan 03 repository will be public.

**Why.**

- Plan 01 has the only moat that compounds (collected history plus maintained parsers)
  and flat running costs. It is the revenue bet.
- Plan 03 has near-zero running cost and the fastest time to a visible artifact. Its
  direct sales ceiling is low, so its main job is distribution and credibility: a public,
  well-tested repo under the Steerwell name is proof of consulting capability and a
  top-of-funnel for both consulting leads and plan 01 subscribers.
- Plan 02 is parked. 2026 search conditions make prose content pages unviable. The
  salvageable part, a free public page per record, is folded into plan 01 as a free tier.
- Plan 04 is parked. Marketplace AI policies are tightening (Teachers Pay Teachers banned
  AI content in October 2026) and the leverage is in marketplace ranking, not production.

**Consequences.**

- Plan 03's revenue model shifts from one-off sales to consulting leads, with paid packs
  as a secondary line. Sections of plan 03 updated accordingly.
- Plan 03's "public repo or not" question is closed: public.
- Plan 01's data pipeline stays private by default. The public plan 03 repo must not
  expose plan 01's source list or parsers.
- Plan 01's go-to-market gains a channel: the plan 03 repo and its README link to the
  data product.

**Still open.** See each plan's "Decisions still open" section, and in particular:
which niche for plan 01, which workflow the first plan 03 artifact demonstrates, and the
license for the public repo.
